# MagicHandy v0.1.0-alpha.86

## Changelog

- Give experimental **VacuGlide** sessions native tempo control: steady levels, six built-in routines, and richer ramps, waves, pulses or sequences according to the chat model's capabilities. Pause/Resume preserves the pattern, and Edge provides an immediate gentle hold.
- Add optional, bounded LLM air adjustments through the shared motion engine. Air control defaults off; Stop cancels pending adjustments and attempts valve closure.
- Redesign **Now playing**, Play controls and the device connection panel with compact Graphite styling, clearer participation status and controls appropriate to the active device.
- Simplify **Autopilot** timing controls. Capable models choose bounded reconsideration times automatically; change rate still controls how frequently decisions may occur. Keep the optional **Session buildup** finish target and the manual motion-method selector.
- Improve elapsed-time context, timed continuations and buildup resets. Resetting or editing buildup discards decisions prepared for the previous goal. Timed requests remain model-dependent, especially with smaller local models; buildup does not automatically stop playback.
- Harden native tempo startup, continuation, response repair, Pause/Resume and Stop against stale or cancelled work. Keep selected companions visibly idle when they cannot participate in native tempo playback.
- VacuGlide remains experimental and has not been physically validated. In mixed sessions it follows a position-capable lead; independent mixed-device tempo control is not included. Autoblow commands, including Stop, require its cloud connection; switch the device off locally if that path fails.
- Windows setup remains unsigned under the reviewed alpha policy. Install over the existing app to preserve data; portable users replace application files while the app is stopped. Source: `v0.1.0-alpha.86`.
