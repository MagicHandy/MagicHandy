# MagicHandy v0.1.0-alpha.54

## Changelog

- Freestyle now evolves as one continuous stroke stream, with Gentle, Balanced, Intense and Custom feels, instead of cycling through repeated catalog segments.
- Shape Freestyle while it runs with independent pace, stroke length, focus, roaming and variety controls, plus an even, quicker-up or quicker-down accent. Live edits ease into the running motion.
- Choose Steady, Slow build, Waves, Edge or Cooldown. Slow build and Cooldown accept 1–240 minutes; changing shape starts a fresh arc, pausing freezes its clock, and Cooldown naturally comes to rest.
- Older saved Gentle and Intense motion styles carry over into the new Freestyle preferences. Queued preset and slider edits preserve the backend's selected values.
- Motion continuity and session shapes have been reviewed in simulation. Physical-device feel and transport latency still need acceptance testing, particularly for occasional wide strokes at high variety.
- Windows setup remains unsigned under the reviewed alpha policy. Install over the existing app to preserve its data; portable users replace the application files while the app is stopped.
