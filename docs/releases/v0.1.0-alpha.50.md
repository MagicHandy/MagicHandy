# MagicHandy v0.1.0-alpha.50

## Changelog

- Chat now reads depth the way you mean it. Asking to go deeper or to bottom out moves the strokes toward the base, and asking for just the tip keeps them near the tip. Replies describe only the depth the motion actually reaches.
- Creative v2 met the requested depth in 44 of 45 test turns, up from 19, and never moved the wrong way.
- Layered moves toward the requested depth more often and follows a request for one stroke width. When its range is narrow, depth can still stop at the range's edge; the LLM Lab is testing replacements.
- New installs start on Creative v2. Existing installs keep the motion mode they already use.
- Switching to another tab or app and back no longer reloads MagicHandy, so an unsent chat message stays in the box and the conversation stays in place.
- The LLM Lab adds three experimental modes that describe strokes by where they turn: Stroke ends, Groove and accents, and Plain words.
- The LLM Lab page is simpler: one row of mode buttons, Live motion and Autopilot switches that take effect at once, one-tap requests such as Deeper and Just the tip, and compact replies with details on demand.
- Compare modes sends one message to every main mode and shows each reply beside a plotted estimate of its motion. Nothing is saved or played.
- Windows setup remains unsigned under the reviewed alpha policy. Install this version over the existing app to preserve its data; portable users replace the application files while the app is stopped.
