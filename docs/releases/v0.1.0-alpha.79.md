# MagicHandy v0.1.0-alpha.79

## Changelog

- Keep Edge's reduced pace and reach while using normal rounded turns. Edge no longer leaves hidden softness in subsequent Layered edits that could cap their effective speed. Authored Lab/library shaping remains honored and visible in context and traces.
- Remove unrequested brief rests from production Simple (Creative v2) variation while retaining its range, pace and phrase variation.
- Let Advanced Layered models request counted cycles in separate regions with continuous connecting travel, and choose different upward/downward travel rates. Preserve counts, direction contrast and shared motion limits; constrained asymmetric patterns can have a lower average pace. Simple and Intermediate do not expose these new controls after failed capability trials.
- Accept phrase edits with recall and redundant removal of nonexistent layers. Improve full-range guidance, resolved-value errors and compiled pace facts so follow-up requests have more accurate context.
- Physical smoothness still needs Original Handy Cloud testing. Two captured Cloud recovery stops remain unexplained; these changes do not claim to eliminate all stutter. Existing vibrator acceptance gaps and Intiface Stop limitations remain open. Models can still misunderstand requests.
- Windows setup remains unsigned under the reviewed alpha policy. Install over the existing app to preserve data; portable users replace application files while the app is stopped. Source: `v0.1.0-alpha.79`.
