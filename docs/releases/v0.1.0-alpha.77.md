# MagicHandy v0.1.0-alpha.77

## Changelog

- Let compatible chat models choose vibrator strength beneath one saved Max. Simple capability in Layered and Intermediate use a smoothly changing level; Advanced can choose independent patterns. Max, model control, motors and playback participation remain your choices.
- Make movement-following vibration respond across the saved Handy speed band. Until the model chooses a level, vibration follows Handy intensity; disabling model control returns to movement following at Max, including after an independent pattern.
- Keep the actual saved rhythm and previous Advanced vibration pattern available after Stop. Fresh motion requests can resume them; ordinary questions and harmless settings echoes leave playback stopped.
- Show Creative v2 as Simple and hide original Creative behind Settings > Chat > Conversation > Offer the original Creative mode. Existing profiles using original Creative keep it available. Layered remains the default.
- Accept equivalent grouped motion edits, matching named geometry controls, and explicit no-change vibration replies without rejecting valid requests. Conflicting controls and unavailable output commands retain their checks.
- Simplify vibrator settings and connection-panel readouts around Max, Model control, Resume now and commanded motor output.
- Vibrator motor feel, minimum usable steps, level glide, Advanced transitions, Stop-to-silence/restart and simultaneous-device failure behavior remain physically unverified. Existing Intiface Stop limitations and the installed-app full-range speed discrepancy remain unresolved; small models can still misunderstand or exceed their response budget.
- Windows setup remains unsigned under the reviewed alpha policy. Install over the existing app to preserve data; portable users replace application files while the app is stopped. Source: `v0.1.0-alpha.77`.
