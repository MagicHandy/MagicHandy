# MagicHandy v0.1.0-alpha.69

## Changelog

- Add counted Layered patterns for external models, such as two shorter strokes followed by a full sweep, with flexible regional coverage and independent pace variation.
- Preserve stroke reach during pace-only follow-ups and give external models clearer feedback about typical coverage and full-range visits.
- Improve transitions between compatible counted patterns, including wider or narrower strokes, while retaining the selected pace and existing motion limits.
- Keep slow full sweeps consistent at either end of the range and give Autopilot check-ins accurate descriptions of the current counted pattern.
- Local-only prompts and ordinary Layered timing remain unchanged. Physical Original Handy / Cloud validation of the new counted patterns is still pending; commanded endpoints do not confirm physical carriage reach.
- Existing device limits remain: Intiface Central may acknowledge Stop without delivering it to The Handy; use Native Bluetooth or Handy Cloud for its device Stop path. Loaded COYOTE output and real vibrator behavior remain unverified, and a native Bluetooth worker crash may require reconnecting both devices.
- Windows setup remains unsigned under the reviewed alpha policy. Install over the existing app to preserve data; portable users replace application files while the app is stopped. Source: `v0.1.0-alpha.69`.
