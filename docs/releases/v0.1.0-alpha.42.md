# MagicHandy v0.1.0-alpha.42

## Changelog

- Improve chat and mode cancellation, shutdown cleanup, and agreement between
  saved settings and the controls shown in the app.
- Cancel Autopilot immediately after an unsafe startup result, preventing a
  second decision while asynchronous shutdown is still waiting to finish.
- Reduce unnecessary database reads and UI updates during active motion,
  keeping conversations and status displays more responsive.
- Speed up video seeking, particularly with large funscripts, and prevent
  delayed playback requests from resuming after Stop or controller loss.
- Preserve major authored peaks and slow subtle movements when removing
  small, rapid funscript jitter.
- Make peak rounding continuous inside the shared motion engine and keep it
  available for dense scripts. The filter panel reports reduced reach, local
  peak timing shifts, shortened windows, skipped corners, and measured zero.
- Keep filter controls aligned with saved backend values. Failed saves leave
  playback stopped instead of automatically resuming.
- Compact the funscript toolbar, with a settings gear at the right of its
  title row and an adjacent timeline-collapse button.
- Rounding can reduce reach and shift individual peaks while preserving the
  overall video clock. Short corners and deliberate hold boundaries can remain
  abrupt; physical feel and alignment still need device validation.
- Windows x64 installer and portable downloads retain the reviewed unsigned
  alpha packaging policy. Install over the existing version to preserve
  settings, chats, and models.
