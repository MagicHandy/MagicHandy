# MagicHandy v0.1.0-alpha.51

## Changelog

- Organize the video library with titles, ratings, tags, notes, filtering and bulk edits. The watch page gives the picture more room, with details and chat available alongside it.
- Choose Script, Chat or Off as the video's motion source. Switching sources waits for the previous source to stop; selecting Script requires Play to begin. Emergency Stop remains available in fullscreen.
- The remote now opens on its own port, normally one above the main app's port. The launch console displays both addresses; `-remote-port` changes the remote port and `-remote-port -1` disables it. HTTPS or reverse-proxy deployments must also expose the remote address they intend to use.
- Remote-only accounts can control an authorized desktop and manage their own password, recovery codes and sessions without access to host administration. Changing an account's access or revoking its grant invalidates stale commands.
- The remote has compact phone and desktop controls, clear pane and list borders, and a chat composer above the Stop bar. Hide its sidebar shortcut in General settings. Remote playback and chat still require the desktop page to remain open and visible.
- Video controls now reject expired commands and commands meant for a previous video or conversation. Seeking, motion-source changes and Stop handle delayed responses more reliably.
- Chat-only replies stop advertising unavailable motion controls or copying stock English answers. Video guidance and Autopilot reply repair respect the selected language, persona and voice.
- The terminal's recent activity survives background polling, and failed HTTP requests appear without enabling Details. Press D to include routine requests.
- New passwords require at least 15 characters. Existing shorter passwords continue to work.
- Windows setup remains unsigned under the reviewed alpha policy. Install over the existing app to preserve its data; portable users replace the application files while the app is stopped.
